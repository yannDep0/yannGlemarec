## Master SIIA


<section class="lecture-notice" markdown="1">

**Parcours Systèmes Interactifs, Intelligents et Autonomes** <BR>
📍 University of Brest, INP Brest, IMT Altantique, France <BR>
🔗 [Master's page, UBO](https://formations.univ-brest.fr/fr/index/sciences-technologies-sante-STS/master-XB/master-informatique-INRBV8Y3/parcours-systemes-interactifs-intelligents-et-autonomes-INRBVLH0.html) <BR>
🔍 [Lectures](https://yanndep0.github.io/yannGlemarec/SIIA/) <BR>

</section>

The Master's degree takes place over 2 years, or 4 semesters. The first year of teaching is in French, while the second is in French and English.

The second year focuses on interactive systems, from their design to their implementation, including tools and methods to make them autonomous. The first semester consists of classes, while the second semester is project-oriented with a technical or scientific review and a 5- to 6-month internship in a research laboratory (with topics proposed by the Lab-STICC) or in a company.

For English-speaking foreign students enrolled in the M2 program as part of a Master by Research, the research project begins in Semester 9, with 3 of the 5 courses offered.
