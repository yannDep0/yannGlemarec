### Internship Overview

Haptic technologies have been used in the entertainment industry, particularly in cinema and video games, for over 50 years. They can improve immersion and user experience through touch-based interfaces, force feedback, and thermal feedback systems.

This internship explores new forms of interaction using haptic feedback in immersive media such as short animated movies and video games. The work focuses on multimodal interactions combining audio, visual, and haptic feedback, and on their effects on user experience, haptic user experience, and engagement.

Building on preliminary user studies, the intern will design, implement, and evaluate new haptic rendering methods in an interactive immersive environment developed for perception studies.

### Tasks and Responsibilities

- Conduct a literature review of multimodal feedback techniques in interactive media.
- Propose and implement novel haptic interactions for interactive movies, games, and mixed reality experiences.
- Design an experimental protocol and conduct a user study to assess the proposed methods.
- Contribute to the writing of a scientific paper for the haptics or virtual reality communities.

### Skills to Be Acquired or Developed

- Conducting a state-of-the-art review and formulating a research question.
- Writing a scientific article following international academic standards.
- Programming in game engines, such as Unreal Engine.
- Understanding and applying haptic technologies and rendering techniques.
- Working with virtual reality and augmented reality systems.
- Designing and running user studies for experimental evaluation.
